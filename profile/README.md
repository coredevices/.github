# Welcome!

We are the company behind Pebble. Learn more at [rePebble.com](https://rePebble.com) and our [blog](https://repebble.com/blog)

The new Pebble smartwatches are powered by 100% open source software. You can download, modify, compile and load your own custom software on your watch. The Pebble mobile app is also open source. That's pretty cool.
